驗證 0 個錯誤 · 0 個警告

模型通過結構與參照檢查。

未模擬項目 — 結果不涵蓋下列內容（由介接器宣告）

  • era resource multiplier: Real production is scaled by era.resourceMultiplier; GDL regenPerMinute is static, so later eras are modelled with the same rates.
  • building purchase/upgrade: A fixed Lv2 reference build is assumed; building cost curves (buildingCost.ts) are not modelled.
  • dynamic capacity: Storage buildings and capacity upgrades (capacity.ts) are collapsed into fixed resource maxima.
  • tribulation randomness: Era 3+ breakthrough is a 50% dice roll (failure drops levels); modelled deterministically as a foundation_pill >= 2 requirement.
  • offline progress / lifespan / reincarnation: Simulation is continuous play time; lifespan exhaustion and daoHeart inheritance are out of scope.
  • crafting crits: The 5% x3 / 10% x2 craft multipliers in craft.ts are not modelled.
  • manual gathering, sect tasks, market, encounters, beast buffs: Player-policy layer not modelled in v0.1.

由 GDL 0.1.0 產生。發現為確定性規則（docs/dashboard-design.md §5），非建議。
